What's New in Yardstik v4.2

🚂 Interactive Yard Track Map & Shift Handoff Mode

Yardstik v4.2 introduces real-time SVG yard track mapping, multi-column Excel spreadsheet ingestion, automated car classification, and dedicated Shift Handoff view modes.

Getting Started at Deployed Locations

  1. Feature Toggle: The Track Map slide is disabled by default to ensure existing locations continue operating smoothly without configuration changes. To enable it, navigate to Extras & Themes in the Control Panel and turn on Yard Track Map Engine.
  2. Upload Track Check: In the Control Panel (Track Management), upload your facility's daily .xlsx or .csv track check file, or download the starter spreadsheet template.
  3. Custom Vector SVG: Optionally upload a custom vector SVG map of your facility layout. The system ships with a high-resolution 11-track starter layout.
  4. Commodity Rules: Customize keyword classification rules and color-coded dashes to match your facility's specific commodities and railcar assignments.
  5. Shift Handoff Mode: Use ?mode=handoff in the Kiosk URL to rotate all operational slides during shift turnover meetings.

Safe Fallbacks & Resiliency

When Track Map is disabled, the Kiosk automatically rotates Slide 1 (Overview) and Slide 2 (Daily Toolbox Talk & Safety Milestones), ensuring zero disruption to existing workflows.
